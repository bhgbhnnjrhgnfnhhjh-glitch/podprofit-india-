3. Add marketplace and payment fees.

❓ FAQ

What is POD?

Print-on-Demand (POD) is a business model where products are produced after an order is received.

What is profit per order?

Profit per order is the amount left after deducting product, shipping, platform, payment and advertising costs from the sale.

What is break-even price?

It is the approximate selling price at which your calculated profit becomes zero.

Does currency selection convert money?

No. Currency selection changes the displayed symbol only. It does not perform currency conversion.

Are marketplace fees exact?

No. Marketplace fees can change. Enter the percentages that apply to your situation.

What is the Business Tracker?

The Business Tracker lets you enter actual monthly revenue and expenses separately from calculator estimates.
