📚 How It Works

1. Enter your selling price.
Add the price you want to charge your customer.
2. Add your product and shipping costs.
These are your main fixed costs per order.
3. Add platform and payment fees.
The calculator estimates fees based on your entered percentages.
4. Add discount and advertising costs.
These reduce the amount you actually keep.
5. Calculate your profit.
The calculator shows profit, margin, break-even price and recommended pricing.
6. Use the Growth Planner.
Set a monthly goal and see the additional profit or orders required.

❓ FAQ

What is POD?

Print-on-Demand is a business model where products are produced after an order is placed.

What does profit per order mean?

It is the estimated amount left from a sale after the costs and fees entered into the calculator.

What is break-even price?

It is the approximate selling price at which your revenue covers the costs and fees entered into the calculator.

Does the calculator convert currencies?

No. Currency selection changes the displayed symbol only. It does not perform live currency conversion.

Are marketplace fees exact?

No. Marketplace presets are estimates. Actual fees can vary by marketplace, country, payment method, taxes and other conditions.

Can I use this for Etsy or Shopify?

Yes. You can select an estimated marketplace fee or enter your own fee percentage.

ℹ️ About PODProfit India

PODProfit India is a simple browser-based calculator designed to help POD sellers understand pricing, costs, profit, break-even points, goals and business forecasts.

🔒 Privacy

PODProfit India does not require an account for basic calculations. Saved calculations are stored locally in your browser when you use the Save feature.

⚠️ Disclaimer

All calculations are estimates based on the information entered by the user. Marketplace fees, taxes, shipping charges, payment charges and advertising costs may vary. Always verify actual costs before making business decisions.
