📚 How It Works

3. Add marketplace and payment fees.

❓ FAQ

What is POD?

Print-on-Demand is a business model where products are produced after an order is placed.

What is profit per order?

It is the estimated amount remaining after the entered costs and fees are deducted.

What is break-even price?

It is the approximate price at which the entered costs and fees are covered.

Does currency selection convert money?

No. Currency selection changes the displayed symbol only.

Are marketplace fees exact?

No. Marketplace presets are estimates. Actual fees can vary.

What is the Business Tracker?

The Business Tracker lets you enter your actual revenue, orders and expenses to calculate real business net profit and margin.

ℹ️ About PODProfit India

PODProfit India is a browser-based tool for POD sellers to understand pricing, costs, profit, goals, forecasts and business performance.

🔒 Privacy

Basic calculations do not require an account. Calculator and tracker data saved using the Save buttons is stored locally in your browser.

⚠️ Disclaimer

All calculations are estimates based on the information entered by the user. Actual marketplace fees, taxes, shipping, payment charges and advertising costs can vary.
