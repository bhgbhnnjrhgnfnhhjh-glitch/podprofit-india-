💡 Smart Pricing Tips

1. Calculate every cost: Include product cost, shipping, marketplace fees, payment fees, discounts and advertising.
2. Don't price only by copying competitors: Your actual costs may be different.
3. Check your profit margin: A higher selling price can sometimes produce a healthier margin.
4. Test multiple prices: Use the price comparison section to compare different selling prices.
5. Watch advertising costs: Paid advertising can reduce profit significantly.
6. Track monthly orders: A profitable product becomes more useful when you know how many orders you need to reach your income goal.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a POD profit calculator?

It helps Print-on-Demand sellers estimate revenue, expenses, profit and profit margin for their products.


Does this calculator convert currencies?

No. The currency selector changes the displayed currency symbol only. It does not perform live exchange-rate conversion.


Are marketplace fees exact?

No. The preset marketplace fees are estimates. Actual fees can vary depending on country, plan, transaction and marketplace rules.


What is break-even price?

Break-even price is the estimated selling price where the order covers the entered costs and fees without profit.


Can I save my calculation?

Yes. The Save Calculation button stores the calculation in your browser.


Does this calculator need an API?

No. This version works directly in the browser using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
