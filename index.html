Complete POD Profit Calculator Guide

What is a POD Profit Calculator?

A POD Profit Calculator helps Print-on-Demand sellers estimate their profit from each order. You can enter your selling price, product cost, shipping cost, platform fees, payment fees, discount and advertising cost to estimate your real profit.

How to Calculate POD Profit

To calculate POD profit, first add your product cost, shipping, platform fees, payment fees and advertising cost. Then subtract the total expenses from the amount you receive after discounts and fees.

Basic formula:

Profit = Net Selling Revenue − Total Order Cost

Your actual profit can change depending on your product cost, marketplace fees, payment fees, discounts, shipping and advertising expenses.

POD Profit Margin

Profit margin shows how much of your net selling revenue remains as profit after the estimated costs are deducted.

Profit Margin = Profit ÷ Net Selling Revenue × 100

How to Choose a POD Selling Price

A good POD selling price should cover your product cost, shipping, marketplace fees, payment fees, discounts and advertising expenses while leaving enough profit for your business.

  • Calculate your complete cost per order.
  • Check your break-even price.
  • Set a target profit per order.
  • Check your desired profit margin.
  • Compare different selling prices.
  • Review your monthly profit goal.

POD Profit Calculator for Indian Sellers

POD sellers in India can use PODProfit India to estimate profit for products such as T-shirts, hoodies, mugs, caps and stickers. Enter your own product and shipping costs for a more useful estimate.

The calculator also lets you compare selling prices, estimate monthly and yearly profit, calculate break-even price and check how many orders may be needed to reach a monthly income target.

Why Calculate POD Profit Before Selling?

A product can have good sales but still produce low profit if costs and fees are not considered. Calculating your estimated profit before choosing a price can help you understand your costs and make better pricing decisions.
