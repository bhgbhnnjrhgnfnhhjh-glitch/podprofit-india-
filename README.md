# PODProfit India

Free browser-based POD profit calculator for Print-on-Demand sellers.

## Includes
- Profit and margin calculator
- Break-even and target pricing
- Dashboard and business tracker
- Goal planner
- Price and product comparison
- Scenario simulator
- Sales and profit forecast
- POD profit guide and FAQ

The calculator uses the values entered by the user. Marketplace fees, taxes, shipping, payment charges and advertising costs can vary. Currency selection changes the displayed symbol only; it does not perform exchange-rate conversion.
